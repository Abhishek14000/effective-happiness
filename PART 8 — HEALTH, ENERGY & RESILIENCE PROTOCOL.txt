8.3 CORE RESILIENCE SYSTEM

Daily non-negotiables:
- Fixed sleep window.
- Structured movement practice.
- Controlled stimulation (screen + caffeine boundaries).
- Minimum one decompression ritual.

Weekly non-negotiables:
- Recovery half-day.
- Workload reset and calendar pruning.
- Emotional processing check-in.

Confidence: High (0.87)

8.4 BURNOUT PREVENTION INDEX

Track weekly:
1) Sleep quality
2) Irritability levels
3) Focus stability
4) Social withdrawal tendency
5) Physical energy baseline

If 3 indicators worsen for 2+ weeks:
- reduce commitments,
- restore sleep first,
- escalate support systems immediately.
