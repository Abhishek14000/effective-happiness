5.2 NATURAL WORK MODES

Primary mode:
- High-responsibility, initiative-led, delivery ownership roles.

Secondary mode:
- Advisory, analytical, process, systems, communication-heavy tracks.

Optimal environment:
- Clear accountability structures.
- Roles where learning + output are both visible.
- Teams that reward consistency over political noise.

Misfit environment signals:
- Constant ambiguity without ownership clarity.
- Repetitive low-growth tasks with no skill compounding.
- Authority structures that block merit-based progression.

Confidence: Moderate-High (0.81)

- Strategy support, policy/process design, institutional systems roles.

Tier B (strong alignment):
- Client-facing advisory where credibility compounds over time.
- Writing, knowledge management, workflow architecture.

Tier C (conditional alignment):
- Entrepreneurial tracks with high volatility (works only with strict routines).

Confidence: Moderate-High (0.80)
