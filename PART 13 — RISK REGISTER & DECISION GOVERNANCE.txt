13.3 DECISION TIERS

- decide quickly with minimal overhead.

Tier 2 (moderate impact):
- require pros/cons, cost/risk analysis, 24-hour pause.

Tier 3 (high impact, long-cycle):
- require written thesis, external feedback, 48-hour cooling, final review at stable emotional baseline.
